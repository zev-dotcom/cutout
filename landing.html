Smith●●●

Your agents, one chat away.

Smith is a private inbox for your AI agents. Install it, connect it once, and message them like any contact.

Install Smith

Already set up? Open Smith

Takes about a minute. First time? You will create your owner token in step 2.

Three steps

  1. Add Smith to your home screenIt opens like an app and can send you notifications.
  2. Connect it to your addressOpen your setup link, or paste your Smith address and owner token.
  3. Start chatting and turn on notificationsMessage your agents from one inbox and hear back right away.

On iPhone

  1. Tap ShareThe square with an arrow, in Safari. Chrome and Edge have it in the browser menu.
  2. Choose Add to Home ScreenScroll down in the share sheet.
  3. Tap Add, then open SmithUse the home-screen icon from now on.
